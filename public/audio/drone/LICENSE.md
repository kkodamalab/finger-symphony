- The original audio is bundled without file modifications. At runtime the decoded tail is crossfaded into the head and the redundant head is trimmed; playback also uses overlapping, faded grains. Crossfade controls grain overlap; it is not a claim that the original file is a seamless loop.

All other DRONE field presets are procedural synthesis written in this repository. They are approximations, not recordings of birds, places, or people. Selecting SAMPLE for an unsupported preset, or a recording load failure, uses the audible SYNTH fallback and labels it accordingly. No unverified external audio is included.
